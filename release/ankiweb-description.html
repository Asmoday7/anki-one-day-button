HoldUp — Extra practice alongside FSRS

Release candidate: 1.0.0-rc.3. Please see the validation record for the current testing status.

Not sure you will remember a card? Schedule another look at a time you choose, alongside your normal Anki reviews.

  1. Show the answer and press HoldUp.
  2. Choose Again, Hard, Good or Easy for the original card as usual.
  3. The extra appearance becomes ready at your chosen time and joins the same reviewer.

HoldUp does not choose a normal rating for you. The original keeps its normal Anki schedule, FSRS history and daily new-card accounting. Extra practice does not submit an FSRS rating, although it can affect what you remember on your next normal review.

  • Choose 1 second to 24 hours, or Next Anki study day using Anki's day-start setting.
  • Each new HoldUp press adds a separate extra appearance; older appointments keep their times.
  • Again in… reschedules the appearance on screen; Done finishes only that appearance.
  • Use normal Undo/Redo for appointments, repeats and completion.
  • See ready appearances in the green review count and a clock badge on the deck list.
  • English and Russian are included in one package and follow Anki's language.

Compatibility: desktop Anki 26.09 and 26.09.3 are the compatibility targets. First-release support is one desktop installation per collection. Full collection exports and Anki backups preserve the extra queue; deck .apkg export does not transfer the complete queue. Live multi-device sync/conflict resolution is unverified. AnkiDroid and AnkiMobile do not run desktop Python add-ons.
